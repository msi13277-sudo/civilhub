Calculators

Quick, transparent tools for common design checks. They run entirely in your browser — nothing is sent to a server. Available for every state.

  • Unit Converter — length, area, volume, flow, mass, pressure/head
  • Scale Converter — drawing measurement ↔ real world distance
  • Slope Converter — %, 1 in X ratio, degrees, rise/run
  • Slope from Dimensions — length + height (or RL / chainage) → slope
  • Height / RL from Slope & Length — slope + length → height change, final RL
  • Pipe Flow (Manning's) — full-bore capacity of a circular pipe
  • Driveway Grade Checker — grade & change-of-grade vs AS/NZS 2890.1 limits
  • Pipe Cover Checker — cover from surface & invert RL vs minimum
  • Cut & Fill Estimator — coming soon
  • Chainage Calculator — coming soon
  • RL & Grade Checker — coming soon
