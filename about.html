About

What this site is, who runs it, and how it is maintained.

What is this site?

Aus Civil Hub is a free reference hub for civil engineering designers, drafters and engineers working in Australia. It collects the links that practitioners look up every day — council DA trackers, planning schemes, standard drawings, GIS and flood mapping, utility as-constructed records and application forms — organised by state, together with a set of quick calculators for common design checks such as slopes, grades, RLs and drawing scales. Queensland is live now, with more states rolling out.

Who runs it?

The site is maintained by a civil designer currently working in the Queensland land development industry. It is an independent, personal project — it is not affiliated with, or endorsed by, any council, utility or government agency. The operator prefers to remain anonymous; the content is intended to speak for itself.

How links are chosen

  • Official sources only — links point to councils, utilities, state government and standards bodies, never to mirrors or re-hosted copies.
  • Checked periodically — links are reviewed on a regular basis and updated when authorities restructure their websites.
  • Latest versions at the source — forms and standard drawings change; always download the current version from the linked official page rather than saving old copies.

About the calculators

The calculators are simple, transparent tools for quick checks — the kind of numbers you would otherwise punch into a hand calculator. They run entirely in your browser and send nothing to a server.

Disclaimer: All calculators and information on this site are provided for reference and checking purposes only. Results must be verified by a qualified engineer before use in any design or construction. This site does not constitute professional engineering advice. Always refer to current Australian Standards, QLD legislation, and relevant authority requirements.
