02 · Maps & GIS

Development Applications (statewide portal)

  • NSW Planning Portal — Application Tracker — search DAs & State Significant applications across NSW by LGA, address or status
  • NSW Planning Portal — Council Application Trackers — index of individual council DA trackers

Planning Controls (LEP / DCP)

  • NSW Planning Portal — Spatial Viewer — enter an address / lot for zoning, Local Environmental Plans (LEPs), Development Control Plans (DCPs) & contribution plans in one place
  • NSW Legislation — Environmental Planning Instruments (LEPs) — full text of LEPs & associated maps (Land Zoning, Height, FSR)

State Mapping / Spatial

  • SIX Maps — cadastral, topographic & aerial imagery (now served through the NSW Spatial Collaboration Portal)
  • NSW Spatial Services — state spatial agency (survey, geodesy & data)

As-Constructed & Underground Assets

  • Before You Dig Australia (BYDA) — free underground asset plans for any site; the standard first stop for NSW utility as-constructed records
  • Sydney Water — Diagrams & Prints — service location prints & sewer service diagrams showing water, wastewater & stormwater assets (ordered via Sydney Water Tap in)
  • Hunter Water — Asset Location Plans — water & sewer main location plans (free via BYDA, or via the Property Self Service Portal)

Flood & Hazard

  • NSW Flood Data Portal — flood studies, floodplain risk management studies & associated data statewide

Environmental & Contamination

  • NSW SEED — Sharing and Enabling Environmental Data portal (environmental & ecological spatial data)
  • EPA — Contaminated Land Record of Notices — record of significantly contaminated land under the Contaminated Land Management (CLM) Act

  • Scale Converter — drawing measurement ↔ real world distance
  • Slope Converter — %, 1 in X ratio, degrees, rise/run
  • Slope from Dimensions — length + height (or RL / chainage) → slope
  • Height / RL from Slope & Length — slope + length → height change, final RL
