Important: This is a screening aid only. The actual AS/NZS 2890.1 standard, ground-vehicle clearance checks (Appendix C templates) and — critically — the relevant council / authority requirements take precedence and are often stricter (many QLD councils prefer a working maximum near 1 in 5 = 20%). A "Review" result flags that a transition or engineering assessment is likely needed, not that the design is illegal. For reference and checking only — verify with a qualified engineer.
