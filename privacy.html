Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 July 2026

This site is a static reference site with no accounts, sign-ups or forms, so it collects no personal information from you directly. It does use Google Analytics to understand, in aggregate, how the site is used. This policy describes both plainly.

What we collect

  • No personal information — the site has no accounts, no sign-up, no forms and no comment system. We do not ask for, or store, any personal data that identifies you.
  • Analytics cookies — we use Google Analytics to measure aggregate traffic (such as which pages are visited). This sets cookies in your browser and collects standard usage data — see the Analytics section below, including how to opt out.
  • Calculators run locally — values you type into the calculators are processed entirely in your browser and are never sent to a server. Links you copy from a calculator contain only the numbers you entered.

Analytics

To understand how the site is used and where to improve it, this site uses Google Analytics 4 (GA4), a web analytics service provided by Google. GA4 sets cookies in your browser and collects information such as the pages you view, the approximate region you visit from (derived from your IP address), your device and browser type, and how you arrived at the site.

This data is used in aggregate to see overall patterns of use — it is not used to identify you as an individual. We do not combine it with any other information, and no advertising or cross-site tracking is configured.

You can opt out of Google Analytics on all websites by installing the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on, or by blocking cookies in your browser's privacy settings. For more on how Google handles this data, see Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites that use its services.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like most web hosts, GitHub may collect standard technical information in its server logs (such as IP addresses) when pages are served. This logging is controlled by GitHub, not by this site — see the GitHub General Privacy Statement for details.

External links

This site links to external websites operated by councils, utilities, government agencies and standards bodies. Once you leave this site, the privacy policy of the site you visit applies. We are not responsible for the content or privacy practices of external sites.

Advertising

The site currently shows no advertising. If advertising is introduced in the future, this policy will be updated beforehand to describe any data collected by the advertising provider, and the "Last updated" date above will change.

Questions

If you have a question about this policy, see the Contact page.
