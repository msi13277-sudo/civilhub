Height change = slope × horizontal length. In 1 in X mode, slope = 1 ÷ X (e.g. 1 in 20 = 5%); use a negative X for downhill. RLs are shown to 3 decimal places as per survey convention. For reference and checking only — verify with a qualified engineer.
