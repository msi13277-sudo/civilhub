Full-bore capacity only

Manning's equation: Q = (1/n) · A · R2/3 · S1/2, with V = Q/A. For a circular pipe flowing just full: A = πD²/4 and R = D/4. This gives the pipe's full-bore capacity — it does not model part-full flow (peak capacity for a circular pipe actually occurs at about 94% depth). Use it as a capacity check only. For reference and checking only — verify with a qualified engineer.

References

Manning's formula (SI form), e.g. Chadwick, Morfett & Borthwick, Hydraulics in Civil and Environmental Engineering; and Queensland Urban Drainage Manual (QUDM), pipe hydraulics. Typical Manning's n values after Chow, Open-Channel Hydraulics (1959) and FHWA HDS-5 (Hydraulic Design of Highway Culverts). Preset values are chosen towards the conservative (rougher) end of the published range so full-bore capacity is not over-estimated: smooth-wall HDPE is taken as 0.011 (FHWA range ≈ 0.009–0.012), cast / ductile iron as 0.014 (new-pipe typical, allowing for service life). Values shown are indicative only — confirm the roughness coefficient appropriate to the pipe material, age and jointing for your design.
